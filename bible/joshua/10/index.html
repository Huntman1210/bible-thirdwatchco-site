Joshua 10

Five Amorite kings attacked Gibeon, and Joshua marched all night from Gilgal to rescue them; the LORD threw the enemy into confusion, cast great hailstones, and the sun stood still until Israel had avenged themselves. Joshua then conquered the southern cities of Makkedah, Libnah, Lachish, Eglon, Hebron, and Debir.
