Zephaniah 1

The LORD will utterly sweep away everything from the face of the land — man and beast, birds and fish, the stumbling blocks with the wicked. He will stretch out His hand against Judah and Jerusalem and cut off every remnant of Baal worship. The great day of the LORD is near — a day of wrath, distress, darkness, and alarm. Neither silver nor gold will deliver them.
