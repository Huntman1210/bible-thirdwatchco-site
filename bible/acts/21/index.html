Acts 21

Paul sailed to Jerusalem despite warnings from the brethren that bonds and afflictions awaited him. He met with James and the elders and agreed to purify himself at the temple, but Jews from Asia stirred up the crowd against him. The Roman soldiers rescued Paul from the mob and allowed him to address the people.
