Luke 24

Women come to the tomb on the first day of the week and find it empty, and two angels announce that Jesus has risen. The risen Jesus walks with two disciples on the road to Emmaus and reveals Himself to them in the breaking of bread. He appears to the gathered disciples, commissions them as witnesses, and ascends into heaven from Bethany.

Some others came with them.
2And they found the stone rolled away from the sepulchre.They found the stone rolled away from the tomb.

4And it came to pass, as they were much perplexed thereabout, behold, two men stood by them in shining garments:While they were puzzled about this, two men in dazzling clothing suddenly stood by them.

9And returned from the sepulchre, and told all these things unto the eleven, and to all the rest.They returned from the tomb and reported all these things to the eleven and to all the rest.
10It was Mary Magdalene, and Joanna, and Mary the mother of James, and other women that were with them, which told these things unto the apostles.These women were Mary Magdalene, Joanna, and Mary the mother of James. The other women with them also told these things to the apostles.

14And they talked together of all these things which had happened.They were talking with each other about everything that had happened.
15And it came to pass, that, while they communed together and reasoned, Jesus himself drew near, and went with them.While they were talking and discussing these things, Jesus Himself drew near and walked with them.
16But their eyes were holden that they should not know him.But their eyes were kept from recognizing Him.
17And he said unto them, What manner of communications are these that ye have one to another, as ye walk, and are sad?He said to them, "What manner of conversation is this that You have with one another as You walk, and are sad?"

31And their eyes were opened, and they knew him; and he vanished out of their sight.Their eyes were opened and they recognized Him. Then He vanished from their sight.

39Behold my hands and my feet, that it is I myself: handle me, and see; for a spirit hath not flesh and bones, as ye see me have."Look at My hands and My feet — it is I Myself. Touch Me and see, for a spirit does not have flesh and bones as You see I have."
