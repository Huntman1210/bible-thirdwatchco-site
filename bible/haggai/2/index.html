Haggai 2

The LORD encourages the builders: though this temple seems as nothing compared to Solomon's, "The desire of all nations shall come, and I will fill this house with glory." The glory of this latter house will be greater than the former. The LORD promises that from this day He will bless them, and He declares Zerubbabel His chosen servant — "I will make you as a signet ring, for I have chosen you."
