Psalms 136

The psalmist gives thanks to the LORD in twenty-six verses, each ending with the refrain that His mercy endures forever. He praises God for creation, the exodus, the conquest of Canaan, and His care for His people in their low estate.

vKing James VersionModern English
1O give thanks unto the LORD; for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good. His mercy endures forever.
2O give thanks unto the God of gods: for his mercy endureth for ever.Give thanks to the God of gods. His mercy endures forever.
3O give thanks to the Lord of lords: for his mercy endureth for ever.Give thanks to the Lord of lords. His mercy endures forever.
4To him who alone doeth great wonders: for his mercy endureth for ever.To Him who alone does great wonders, His mercy endures forever.
5To him that by wisdom made the heavens: for his mercy endureth for ever.Who by His understanding made the heavens, His mercy endures forever.
6To him that stretched out the earth above the waters: for his mercy endureth for ever.Who spread out the earth upon the waters, His mercy endures forever.
7To him that made great lights: for his mercy endureth for ever:Who made the great lights — his mercy endures forever.
8The sun to rule by day: for his mercy endureth for ever:The sun to govern the day, His mercy endures forever.
9The moon and stars to rule by night: for his mercy endureth for ever.The moon and stars to govern the night; His mercy endures forever.
10To him that smote Egypt in their firstborn: for his mercy endureth for ever:To Him who struck down the firstborn of Egypt, His mercy endures forever.
11And brought out Israel from among them: for his mercy endureth for ever:And brought Israel out from among them, His mercy endures forever.
12With a strong hand, and with a stretched out arm: for his mercy endureth for ever.With a mighty hand and outstretched arm; His mercy endures forever.
13To him which divided the Red sea into parts: for his mercy endureth for ever:To Him who divided the Red Sea asunder, His mercy endures forever.
14And made Israel to pass through the midst of it: for his mercy endureth for ever:And brought Israel through the midst of it, His mercy endures forever.
15But overthrew Pharaoh and his host in the Red sea: for his mercy endureth for ever.But swept Pharaoh and his army into the Red Sea; his mercy endures forever.
16To him which led his people through the wilderness: for his mercy endureth for ever.To him who led his people through the wilderness; his mercy endures forever.
17To him which smote great kings: for his mercy endureth for ever:To him who struck down great kings, his mercy endures forever.
18And slew famous kings: for his mercy endureth for ever:And killed mighty kings — his mercy endures forever.
19Sihon king of the Amorites: for his mercy endureth for ever:Sihon king of the Amorites, his mercy endures forever.
20And Og the king of Bashan: for his mercy endureth for ever:And Og king of Bashan — his mercy endures forever.
21And gave their land for an heritage: for his mercy endureth for ever:And gave their land as an inheritance, his mercy endures forever.
22Even an heritage unto Israel his servant: for his mercy endureth for ever.An inheritance to his servant Israel; his mercy endures forever.
23Who remembered us in our low estate: for his mercy endureth for ever:He remembered us in our low condition, his mercy endures forever.
24And hath redeemed us from our enemies: for his mercy endureth for ever.And freed us from our enemies. His mercy endures forever.
25Who giveth food to all flesh: for his mercy endureth for ever.He gives food to every creature. His mercy endures forever.
26O give thanks unto the God of heaven: for his mercy endureth for ever.Give thanks to the God of heaven. His mercy endures forever.
