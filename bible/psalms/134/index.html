Psalms 134

The psalmist calls the servants of the LORD who stand by night in the house of the LORD to lift up their hands in the sanctuary and bless the LORD. He pronounces a blessing: may the LORD who made heaven and earth bless you from Zion.

vKing James VersionModern English
1Behold, bless ye the LORD, all ye servants of the LORD, which by night stand in the house of the LORD.Praise the LORD, all You servants of the LORD Who minister by night in the house of the LORD.
2Lift up your hands in the sanctuary, and bless the LORD.Lift up Your hands in the sanctuary and praise the LORD.
3The LORD that made heaven and earth bless thee out of Zion.May the LORD bless You from Zion, He who is the Maker of heaven and earth.
