Psalms 149

The psalmist calls the saints to sing a new song to the LORD and rejoice in their Maker and King with dancing, timbrel, and harp. He gives them a two-edged sword to execute vengeance on the nations and bind their kings — this honor belongs to all His saints.

vKing James VersionModern English
1Praise ye the LORD. Sing unto the LORD a new song, and his praise in the congregation of saints.Praise the LORD. Sing to the LORD a new song, His praise in the congregation of saints.
2Let Israel rejoice in him that made him: let the children of Zion be joyful in their King.Let Israel rejoice in their Maker; let the people of Zion be glad in their King.
3Let them praise his name in the dance: let them sing praises unto him with the timbrel and harp.Let them praise His name with dancing and make music to Him with timbrel and harp.
4For the LORD taketh pleasure in his people: he will beautify the meek with salvation.For the LORD takes delight in His people; He crowns the humble with victory.
5Let the saints be joyful in glory: let them sing aloud upon their beds.Let the saints be joyful in glory; let them sing aloud on their beds.
6Let the high praises of God be in their mouth, and a twoedged sword in their hand;May the praise of God be in their mouths and a double-edged sword in their hands,
7To execute vengeance upon the heathen, and punishments upon the people;to inflict vengeance on the nations and punishment on the peoples,
8To bind their kings with chains, and their nobles with fetters of iron;to bind their kings with fetters, their nobles with shackles of iron,
9To execute upon them the judgment written: this honour have all his saints. Praise ye the LORD.to execute on them the judgment written; this honor have all His saints. Praise the LORD.
