Exodus 12

The LORD instituted the Passover: each household was to take a lamb without blemish, kill it at twilight, and strike its blood on the doorposts and lintel so that the destroyer would pass over when he struck Egypt's firstborn. At midnight the LORD killed every firstborn in Egypt, from Pharaoh's son to the captive's son, and there was a great cry; Pharaoh urgently sent Israel away that very night. About 600,000 men on foot besides children departed, along with a mixt multitude, taking their unleavened dough and the silver and gold of the Egyptians.
