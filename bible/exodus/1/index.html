Exodus 1

The children of Israel multiplied greatly in Egypt after Joseph's death until a new Pharaoh who did not know Joseph arose and enslaved them with bitter labor, forcing them to build the store cities of Pithom and Raamses. When oppression failed to slow their growth, Pharaoh ordered the Hebrew midwives to kill every newborn son, but they feared God and disobeyed; finally Pharaoh commanded all his people to cast every Hebrew son into the Nile.

13And the Egyptians made the children of Israel to serve with rigour:So the Egyptians made the children of Israel serve with harshness.
