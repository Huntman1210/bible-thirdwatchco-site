Exodus 16

The whole congregation murmured against Moses and Aaron in the wilderness of Sin, wishing they had died in Egypt where they had bread to eat. The LORD sent quail in the evening and manna from heaven each morning — a small, round, white substance tasting like wafers with honey — commanding them to gather a daily portion but double on the sixth day, for the seventh day was a Sabbath rest. Some disobeyed and it bred worms; others went out on the Sabbath and found nothing; Israel ate manna for forty years until they reached Canaan.
