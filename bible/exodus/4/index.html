Exodus 4

God gave Moses three signs to prove his authority — the staff turned serpent, the leprous hand, and water turned to blood — and when Moses protested that he was slow of speech, the LORD angrily appointed Aaron as his spokesman. Moses returned to Egypt with his family; on the way the LORD sought to kill him until Zipporah circumcised their son. Aaron met Moses at the mountain of God, and together they gathered Israel's elders, performed the signs, and the people believed and worshiped.
