Exodus 31

The LORD named Bezaleel of the tribe of Judah and Aholiab of the tribe of Dan as the master craftsmen for building the tabernacle, filling them with the Spirit of God in wisdom, understanding, and skill. The LORD restated the command to keep the Sabbath as a perpetual sign between himself and Israel, declaring that whoever works on it shall be put to death. When he finished speaking with Moses on Sinai, God gave him the two tables of testimony, written with the finger of God.
