Exodus 6

The LORD reassured Moses, revealing his name JEHOVAH and renewing his covenant promises to bring Israel out of Egypt with great judgments and give them the land sworn to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. The genealogy of Reuben, Simeon, and Levi is given, tracing the Levitical line through Kohath and Amram to Moses and Aaron, and through Aaron to his sons Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. The LORD commanded Moses and Aaron again to go to Pharaoh despite Moses' protest that he was of uncircumcised lips.

I will be Your God, and You shall know that I am the LORD Your God, Who has brought You out from under the burdens of the Egyptians.

I will give it to you for a possession. I am the LORD.'
