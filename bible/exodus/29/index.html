Exodus 29

The consecration ceremony for Aaron and his sons is described: they are to be washed, clothed in the priestly garments, and anointed; a bull is offered as a sin offering and two rams as a burnt offering and ordination offering over seven days. The LORD commands a continual burnt offering of two lambs daily, one in the morning and one at evening, at the door of the tent of meeting. God promises to sanctify the tabernacle and dwell among the children of Israel and be their God.
