Exodus 38

The bronze altar of burnt offering and the bronze basin were constructed, and the court of the tabernacle was set up with its pillars, hangings, and gate screen. An accounting of the materials used is given: the gold from the offering totaled twenty-nine talents and 730 shekels, the silver came from the atonement money of 603,550 men at a half-shekel each, and the bronze of the offering was seventy talents and 2,400 shekels.

1And he made the altar of burnt offering of shittim wood: five cubits was the length thereof, and five cubits the breadth thereof; it was foursquare; and three cubits the height thereof.He made the altar of burnt offering of acacia wood. He made all its utensils of bronze.
4And he made for the altar a brasen grate of network under the compass thereof beneath unto the midst of it.he made for the altar a grating, a network of bronze, under its ledge, extending halfway down.
5And he cast four rings for the four ends of the grate of brass, to be places for the staves.He cast four rings on the four corners of the bronze grating as holders for the poles.
6And he made the staves of shittim wood, and overlaid them with brass.He made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with bronze.
7And he put the staves into the rings on the sides of the altar, to bear it withal; he made the altar hollow with boards.he put the poles through the rings on the sides of the altar to carry it with them. He made it hollow, with boards.
