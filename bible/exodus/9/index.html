Exodus 9

The LORD struck Egypt's livestock with a deadly plague that killed their horses, donkeys, camels, cattle, and sheep, but none of Israel's animals died; still Pharaoh's heart was hardened. Next Moses threw soot toward heaven and it became boils breaking out on man and beast throughout Egypt. Then the LORD sent a devastating hailstorm that destroyed everything in the field — except in Goshen — and Pharaoh confessed he had sinned, but hardened his heart again once the hail stopped.
