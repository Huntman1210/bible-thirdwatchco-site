Exodus 20

God spoke the Ten Commandments from Mount Sinai: no other gods, no graven images, do not take the LORD's name in vain, keep the Sabbath, honor father and mother, do not kill, do not commit adultery, do not steal, do not bear false witness, do not covet. The people stood far off, trembling at the thunder and fire, and begged Moses to speak to them instead of God. The LORD gave instructions for building altars of earth or uncut stone, forbidding steps that would expose nakedness.
