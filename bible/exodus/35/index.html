Exodus 35

Moses assembled the congregation and restated the command to keep the Sabbath, then called for a freewill offering of materials for the tabernacle — gold, silver, bronze, fabrics, skins, wood, oil, spices, and stones. The people brought willingly, and Moses identified Bezaleel and Aholiab as the craftsmen God had filled with his Spirit to design and build the tabernacle and all its furnishings.

1And Moses gathered all the congregation of the children of Israel together, and said unto them, These are the words which the LORD hath commanded, that ye should do them.Moses assembled all the congregation of the children of Israel and said to them, 'These are the things that the LORD has commanded You to do.
2Six days shall work be done, but on the seventh day there shall be to you an holy day, a sabbath of rest to the LORD: whosoever doeth work therein shall be put to death.Six days work shall be done, but on the seventh day You shall have a Sabbath of solemn rest, holy to the LORD. Whoever does any work on it shall be put to death.

4And Moses spake unto all the congregation of the children of Israel, saying, This is the thing which the LORD commanded, saying,Moses said to all the congregation of the children of Israel, 'This is the thing that the LORD has commanded.

14The candlestick also for the light, and his furniture, and his lamps, with the oil for the light,the lampstand also for the light, with its utensils and its lamps, and the oil for the light;

20And all the congregation of the children of Israel departed from the presence of Moses.Then all the congregation of the children of Israel departed from the presence of Moses.
