Exodus 40

The LORD commanded Moses to set up the tabernacle on the first day of the first month; Moses erected the structure, placed the ark inside the most holy place, hung the veil, arranged the table, lampstand, and golden altar, and anointed everything with oil. He consecrated Aaron and his sons and set up the court around the tabernacle. When the work was finished, the cloud covered the tent of meeting and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle so that Moses could not enter; the cloud of the LORD was on the tabernacle by day and fire by night throughout all Israel's journeys.
