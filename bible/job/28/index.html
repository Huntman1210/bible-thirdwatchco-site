Job 28

Job speaks a great poem on wisdom, declaring that man can mine silver and gold from the earth but cannot discover where wisdom dwells. It is hidden from the eyes of all living and cannot be purchased with gold or precious stones. God alone understands its way: "The fear of the Lord, that is wisdom."
