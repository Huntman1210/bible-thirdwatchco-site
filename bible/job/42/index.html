Job 42

Job repented in dust and ashes, confessing that he had spoken of things too wonderful for him. The LORD rebuked the three friends for not speaking rightly and commanded them to offer sacrifices while Job prayed for them. God restored Job's fortunes double and blessed his latter end more than his beginning.
