Job 38

The LORD answers Job out of the whirlwind, demanding to know where Job was when He laid the foundations of the earth. God interrogates Job with unanswerable questions about the sea, the dawn, the storehouses of snow, the constellations, and the rain -- revealing that Job cannot comprehend even the natural order, let alone the moral one.
