Jonah 3

The word of the LORD came to Jonah a second time: "Go to Nineveh." Jonah obeyed and preached, "Yet forty days, and Nineveh shall be overthrown." The entire city believed God — from the king to the least of them — and put on sackcloth and fasted. God saw their repentance and relented from the disaster.
