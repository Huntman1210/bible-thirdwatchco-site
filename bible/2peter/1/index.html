2 Peter 1

Peter urges believers to add to their faith virtue, knowledge, temperance, patience, godliness, brotherly kindness, and charity, so they will never stumble. He affirms that he was an eyewitness of Christ's majesty on the holy mount, and declares that no prophecy of scripture is of any private interpretation, for holy men of God spoke as they were moved by the Holy Ghost.
