2 Peter 2

Peter warns that false teachers will bring in damnable heresies, even denying the Lord that bought them, and many will follow their destructive ways. He cites God's judgment on the fallen angels, the ancient world through the flood, and Sodom and Gomorrah as proof that the Lord knows how to deliver the godly out of temptations and to reserve the unjust unto the day of judgment to be punished.
