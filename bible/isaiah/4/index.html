Isaiah 4

Isaiah prophesies that in that day seven women will take hold of one man. The Branch of the LORD will be beautiful and glorious for the survivors of Israel, and the LORD will create over Mount Zion a cloud by day and a fire by night as a covering of glory.

vKing James VersionModern English
1And in that day seven women shall take hold of one man, saying, We will eat our own bread, and wear our own apparel: only let us be called by thy name, to take away our reproach.In that day seven women shall take hold of one man, saying, "We will eat our own bread and wear our own clothes — only let us be called by Your name; take away our shame."
2In that day shall the branch of the LORD be beautiful and glorious, and the fruit of the earth shall be excellent and comely for them that are escaped of Israel.In that day the Branch of the LORD shall be beautiful and glorious, and the fruit of the land shall be the pride and glory of the survivors of Israel.
3And it shall come to pass, that he that is left in Zion, and he that remaineth in Jerusalem, shall be called holy, even every one that is written among the living in Jerusalem:It shall come to pass that He who is left in Zion and remains in Jerusalem shall be called holy — everyone recorded among the living in Jerusalem —
4When the Lord shall have washed away the filth of the daughters of Zion, and shall have purged the blood of Jerusalem from the midst thereof by the spirit of judgment, and by the spirit of burning.when the Lord has washed away the filth of the daughters of Zion and cleansed the bloodstains of Jerusalem from its midst by a spirit of judgment and by a spirit of burning.
5And the LORD will create upon every dwelling place of mount Zion, and upon her assemblies, a cloud and smoke by day, and the shining of a flaming fire by night: for upon all the glory shall be a defence.Then the LORD will create over every home on Mount Zion and over her assemblies a cloud of smoke by day and a bright flame of fire by night. Over all the glory there will be a canopy.
6And there shall be a tabernacle for a shadow in the daytime from the heat, and for a place of refuge and for a covert from storm and from rain.There shall be a shelter for shade from the heat by day, and a refuge and hiding place from the storm and rain.
