Genesis 27

Isaac, old and blind, sent Esau to hunt game so he could bless him, but Rebekah and Jacob deceived Isaac with goat skins and savory food, and Jacob received the firstborn's blessing. When Esau returned and the deception was discovered, Isaac trembled and Esau wept bitterly, receiving only a lesser blessing. Esau resolved to kill Jacob, so Rebekah urged Jacob to flee to her brother Laban in Haran.
