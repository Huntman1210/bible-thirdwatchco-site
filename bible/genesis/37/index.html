Genesis 37

Jacob loved Joseph above all his sons and made him a coat of many colors, which provoked his brothers' hatred; Joseph's dreams of his family bowing down to him deepened their resentment. His brothers conspired to kill him but instead sold him to Midianite merchants for twenty pieces of silver; the merchants took Joseph to Egypt and sold him to Potiphar, an officer of Pharaoh. The brothers dipped Joseph's coat in goat's blood and deceived Jacob into believing his son was dead.
