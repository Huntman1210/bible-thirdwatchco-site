Genesis 44

Joseph planted his silver cup in Benjamin's sack and sent his steward to overtake the brothers; when the cup was found, all the brothers returned to Joseph's house in dismay. Judah delivered an impassioned plea, recounting Jacob's love for Benjamin and offering himself as a slave in Benjamin's place so the boy could return to their father.
