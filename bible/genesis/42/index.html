Genesis 42

Jacob sent ten of his sons to buy grain in Egypt, but kept Benjamin home; Joseph recognized his brothers but concealed his identity, accused them of being spies, and imprisoned them for three days. He kept Simeon as a hostage and sent the others back to Canaan with grain and their money secretly returned in their sacks, demanding they bring Benjamin on their next trip.
