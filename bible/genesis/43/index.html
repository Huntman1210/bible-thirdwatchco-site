Genesis 43

When the grain ran out, Judah persuaded Jacob to let Benjamin go to Egypt with them, pledging his own life as surety. Joseph saw Benjamin, had them brought to his house, released Simeon, and hosted a feast where Benjamin received five times the portion of the others.
