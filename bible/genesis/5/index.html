Genesis 5

The descendants of Adam are listed from Seth to Noah, recording each patriarch's age at the birth of his firstborn and at death. Enoch walked with God and was taken — he did not die. Noah was born to Lamech, and Noah begat Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
