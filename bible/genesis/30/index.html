Genesis 30

Rachel, barren and envious of Leah, gave her maid Bilhah to Jacob; Bilhah bore Dan and Naphtali, and Leah's maid Zilpah bore Gad and Asher. Leah bore Issachar, Zebulun, and a daughter Dinah; then God remembered Rachel, and she bore Joseph. Jacob bargained with Laban over wages, breeding the speckled and spotted livestock for himself, and grew exceedingly prosperous.
