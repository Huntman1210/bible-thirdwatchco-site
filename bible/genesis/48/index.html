Genesis 48

Jacob adopted Joseph's two sons Ephraim and Manasseh as his own, giving them equal standing with Reuben and Simeon among the tribes of Israel. When blessing them, Jacob deliberately crossed his hands and placed his right hand on the younger Ephraim's head, declaring that Ephraim would be greater than his older brother Manasseh.
