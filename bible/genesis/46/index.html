Genesis 46

The descendants of Israel who went down to Egypt are listed, seventy persons in all, organized by the sons of Leah, Zilpah, Rachel, and Bilhah. God spoke to Jacob at Beersheba, promising to make him a great nation in Egypt, and Joseph met his father in Goshen where they wept together.
