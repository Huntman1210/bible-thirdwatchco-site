Genesis 22

God tested Abraham by commanding him to offer Isaac as a burnt offering on a mountain in the land of Moriah. Abraham obeyed, bound Isaac on the altar, and raised the knife, but the angel of the LORD stopped him and provided a ram caught in a thicket instead. God swore to bless Abraham and multiply his seed because he had not withheld his only son.
