Genesis 45

Joseph could no longer restrain himself and revealed his identity to his brothers, weeping aloud, telling them not to be angry with themselves because God had sent him ahead to preserve life during the famine. Pharaoh invited the whole family to settle in the best of Egypt's land, and the brothers returned to Canaan with wagons and gifts to bring Jacob down to Egypt.
