Genesis 41

Two years later Pharaoh dreamed of seven fat cows devoured by seven thin cows and seven plump ears of grain swallowed by seven thin ears; none of his wise men could interpret the dreams. The cupbearer finally remembered Joseph, who was brought from prison and told Pharaoh the dreams meant seven years of plenty followed by seven years of famine. Pharaoh set Joseph over all Egypt to store grain during the years of abundance.
