Genesis 38

Judah married a Canaanite woman and had three sons; his firstborn Er died for his wickedness, and Onan was also slain for refusing to raise up seed for his brother. Judah withheld his third son Shelah from Tamar, so she disguised herself, lay with Judah by the roadside, and conceived twins. When Judah learned the truth he declared her more righteous than himself; she bore Pharez and Zarah.
