Genesis 24

Abraham sent his servant to Mesopotamia to find a wife for Isaac from among his kinsmen, not from the Canaanites. The servant prayed at the well and God guided him to Rebekah, who watered his camels; her family agreed to the match. Rebekah returned to Canaan and became Isaac's wife, and he loved her.
