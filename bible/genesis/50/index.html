Genesis 50

Joseph had his father embalmed, and a great company of Egyptians and Israelites carried Jacob's body to Canaan and buried him in the cave of Machpelah. After returning to Egypt, Joseph's brothers feared he would take revenge, but Joseph told them not to fear, declaring that what they meant for evil God meant for good to save many lives. Joseph lived 110 years, saw his great-grandchildren, and before dying made Israel swear to carry his bones out of Egypt when God visited them.
