Hebrews 11

The author defines faith as the substance of things hoped for and the evidence of things not seen, then recounts the great cloud of witnesses who lived by faith. By faith Abel offered, by faith Noah built the ark, by faith Abraham obeyed, by faith Moses chose to suffer with the people of God, and all these obtained a good report through faith though they did not receive the promise.
