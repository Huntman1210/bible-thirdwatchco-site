Hebrews 5

The author explains the role of the high priest, showing that Christ did not glorify Himself but was called by God, a priest for ever after the order of Melchisedec. He rebukes the readers for being dull of hearing, still needing milk rather than strong meat, when by this time they ought to be teachers.
