Hebrews 3

The author argues that Christ is worthy of more glory than Moses, as the builder of the house has more honour than the house itself. He warns against hardening the heart as Israel did in the wilderness, quoting Psalm 95, and urges believers to exhort one another daily lest any be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin.
