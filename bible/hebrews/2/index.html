Hebrews 2

The author warns against neglecting so great a salvation and explains why Christ was made a little lower than the angels — to taste death for every man. He argues that because the children partake of flesh and blood, Christ also took part of the same, that through death He might destroy him that had the power of death, that is, the devil, and deliver those who through fear of death were all their lifetime subject to bondage.
