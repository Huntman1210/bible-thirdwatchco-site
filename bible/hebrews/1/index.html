Hebrews 1

The author argues that God, who spoke in times past through the prophets, has in these last days spoken to us by His Son, who is the brightness of His glory and the express image of His person. He demonstrates the Son's superiority over the angels by citing seven Old Testament passages proving that the Son is worshiped by angels and possesses an eternal throne.
