Hebrews 9

The author describes the earthly tabernacle and its ordinances of worship, then argues that Christ entered not into a man-made sanctuary but into heaven itself, appearing in the presence of God for us. He declares that without shedding of blood there is no remission, and that Christ was once offered to bear the sins of many, and unto them that look for Him shall He appear the second time without sin unto salvation.
