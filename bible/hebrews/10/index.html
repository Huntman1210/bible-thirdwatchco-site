Hebrews 10

The author argues that the law's sacrifices, offered year after year, can never make the worshippers perfect, but by one offering Christ has perfected for ever them that are sanctified. He warns that if we sin wilfully after receiving the knowledge of the truth, there remains no more sacrifice for sins but a certain fearful looking for of judgment, and exhorts believers not to forsake the assembling of themselves together.
