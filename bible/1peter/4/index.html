1 Peter 4

Peter urges believers to arm themselves with the mind of Christ, to cease from sin, and not to think it strange when the fiery trial comes as though something unusual were happening. He teaches that judgment must begin at the house of God, and that those who suffer according to the will of God should commit the keeping of their souls to Him in well doing as unto a faithful Creator.
