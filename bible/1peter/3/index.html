1 Peter 3

Peter instructs wives to be in subjection to their own husbands, adorning themselves with the hidden man of the heart, and commands husbands to dwell with their wives according to knowledge, giving honour unto the weaker vessel. He teaches that Christ also suffered once for sins, the just for the unjust, that He might bring us to God, and urges believers to be always ready to give an answer for the hope that is in them with meekness and fear.
