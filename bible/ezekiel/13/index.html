Ezekiel 13

The LORD condemns the false prophets of Israel who prophesy peace when there is no peace, whitewashing a flimsy wall. He also denounces the women who sew magic bands and veils to hunt souls for handfuls of barley and pieces of bread.

vKing James VersionModern English
1And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,The word of the LORD came to me:
2Son of man, prophesy against the prophets of Israel that prophesy, and say thou unto them that prophesy out of their own hearts, Hear ye the word of the LORD;"Son of man, prophesy against the prophets of Israel who are now prophesying. Say to those who prophesy from their own imagination, 'Hear the word of the LORD!'"
3Thus saith the Lord GOD; Woe unto the foolish prophets, that follow their own spirit, and have seen nothing!"This is what the Lord GOD says: How terrible it will be for the foolish prophets who follow their own spirit and have seen nothing!"
4O Israel, thy prophets are like the foxes in the deserts."Israel, Your prophets are like jackals among ruins."
5Ye have not gone up into the gaps, neither made up the hedge for the house of Israel to stand in the battle in the day of the LORD."You have not gone up to the breaks in the wall or repaired it for the house of Israel so it will stand firm in battle on the day of the LORD."
6They have seen vanity and lying divination, saying, The LORD saith: and the LORD hath not sent them: and they have made others to hope that they would confirm the word."Their visions are false and their divinations a lie. They say, 'The LORD declares,' but the LORD has not sent them. Yet they expect their word to be confirmed."
7Have ye not seen a vain vision, and have ye not spoken a lying divination, whereas ye say, The LORD saith it; albeit I have not spoken?"Have You not seen false visions and spoken lying divinations when You say, 'The LORD declares,' though I have not spoken?"
8Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD; Because ye have spoken vanity, and seen lies, therefore, behold, I am against you, saith the Lord GOD."Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because You have spoken false visions and seen lies, I am against You, declares the Lord GOD."
9And mine hand shall be upon the prophets that see vanity, and that divine lies: they shall not be in the assembly of my people, neither shall they be written in the writing of the house of Israel, neither shall they enter into the land of Israel; and ye shall know that I am the Lord GOD."My hand will be against the prophets who see false visions and give lying predictions. They will not belong to the council of My people or be listed in the records of Israel or enter the land of Israel. You will know that I am the Lord GOD."
10Because, even because they have seduced my people, saying, Peace; and there was no peace; and one built up a wall, and, lo, others daubed it with untempered morter:"Because they have led My people astray by saying 'Peace' when there is no peace — and because when a flimsy wall is built, they coat it with whitewash —"
11Say unto them which daub it with untempered morter, that it shall fall: there shall be an overflowing shower; and ye, O great hailstones, shall fall; and a stormy wind shall rend it."tell those who coat it with whitewash that it will fall. A torrential rain will come. I will send great hailstones crashing down, and a violent wind will tear it apart."
12Lo, when the wall is fallen, shall it not be said unto you, Where is the daubing wherewith ye have daubed it?"When the wall collapses, people will say to You, 'Where is the whitewash You covered it with?'"
13Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD; I will even rend it with a stormy wind in my fury; and there shall be an overflowing shower in mine anger, and great hailstones in my fury to consume it."Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: In My wrath I will send a violent wind. In My anger a torrential rain will fall, and in My fury great hailstones will pound it to destruction."
14So will I break down the wall that ye have daubed with untempered morter, and bring it down to the ground, so that the foundation thereof shall be discovered, and it shall fall, and ye shall be consumed in the midst thereof: and ye shall know that I am the LORD."I will tear down the wall You have covered with whitewash and level it to the ground so that its foundation will be exposed. When it falls, You will be destroyed in it. You will know that I am the LORD."
15Thus will I accomplish my wrath upon the wall, and upon them that have daubed it with untempered morter, and will say unto you, The wall is no more, neither they that daubed it;"I will spend My wrath on the wall and on those who covered it with whitewash. I will say to You, 'The wall is gone, and so are those who whitewashed it.'"
16To wit, the prophets of Israel which prophesy concerning Jerusalem, and which see visions of peace for her, and there is no peace, saith the Lord GOD."These are the prophets of Israel who prophesied to Jerusalem and saw visions of peace for her when there was no peace, declares the Lord GOD."
17Likewise, thou son of man, set thy face against the daughters of thy people, which prophesy out of their own heart; and prophesy thou against them,"Now, Son of man, set Your face against the daughters of Your people who prophesy from their own imagination. Prophesy against them."
18And say, Thus saith the Lord GOD; Woe to the women that sew pillows to all armholes, and make kerchiefs upon the head of every stature to hunt souls! Will ye hunt the souls of my people, and will ye save the souls alive that come unto you?"Say: This is what the Lord GOD says: How terrible it will be for the women who sew magic bands on every wrist and make veils for people of every height to trap souls. Will You trap the souls of My people and keep Your own souls alive?"
19And will ye pollute me among my people for handfuls of barley and for pieces of bread, to slay the souls that should not die, and to save the souls alive that should not live, by your lying to my people that hear your lies?"You have defiled Me among My people for handfuls of barley and pieces of bread. By lying to My people who love a lie, You have killed those who should not die and kept alive those who should not live."
20Wherefore thus saith the Lord GOD; Behold, I am against your pillows, wherewith ye there hunt the souls to make them fly, and I will tear them from your arms, and will let the souls go, even the souls that ye hunt to make them fly."Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I am against Your magic bands with which You trap people like birds. I will tear them from Your arms and set free the people you have trapped."
21Your kerchiefs also will I tear, and deliver my people out of your hand, and they shall be no more in your hand to be hunted; and ye shall know that I am the LORD."I will tear off Your veils and rescue My people from Your hands. They will no longer fall prey to Your power. You will know that I am the LORD."
22Because with lies ye have made the heart of the righteous sad, whom I have not made sad; and strengthened the hands of the wicked, that he should not return from his wicked way, by promising him life:"Because you disheartened the righteous with your lies when I had not caused them grief, and because you encouraged the wicked not to turn from their evil ways and so preserved their lives —"
23Therefore ye shall see no more vanity, nor divine divinations: for I will deliver my people out of your hand: and ye shall know that I am the LORD."therefore You will no longer see false visions or give predictions. I will rescue My people from Your hands. You will know that I am the LORD."
