Numbers 4

The LORD gives instructions for the service of the Levite clans when the camp moves. The Kohathites carry the holy objects after Aaron's sons cover them, the Gershonites carry the curtains and coverings, and the Merarites carry the frames and pillars; the total number of Levites in service is 8,580.
