Numbers 32

The tribes of Reuben and Gad, who have large herds, ask Moses for the land east of the Jordan as their inheritance. Moses rebukes them but agrees on the condition that their armed men cross the Jordan and fight alongside the other tribes until Canaan is conquered; the half-tribe of Manasseh also settles east of the Jordan.
