Numbers 14

The people weep, rebel, and threaten to choose a new leader to return to Egypt. The LORD threatens to destroy them, but Moses intercedes; God pardons the nation but decrees that the entire adult generation except Caleb and Joshua will die in the wilderness over forty years. The people then presumptuously try to invade Canaan and are defeated.
