Numbers 1

The LORD commands Moses to take a census of all men twenty years old and older who are able to go to war, tribe by tribe. The total count of Israel's fighting men is 603,550; the Levites are excluded from the military census and assigned to care for the tabernacle.

vKing James VersionModern English
1And the LORD spake unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tabernacle of the congregation, on the first day of the second month, in the second year after they were come out of the land of Egypt, saying,The LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tent of meeting, on the first day of the second month, in the second year after they had come out of the land of Egypt, saying,
2Take ye the sum of all the congregation of the children of Israel, after their families, by the house of their fathers, with the number of their names, every male by their polls;'Take a census of all the congregation of the children of Israel, by clans and ancestral houses, according to the number of names, every male, head by head.
3From twenty years old and upward, all that are able to go forth to war in Israel: thou and Aaron shall number them by their armies.From twenty years old and upward, all in Israel who are able to go to war, you and Aaron shall list them, company by company.
4And with you there shall be a man of every tribe; every one head of the house of his fathers.there shall be with you a man from each tribe, each man being the head of the house of his fathers.
5And these are the names of the men that shall stand with you: of the tribe of Reuben; Elizur the son of Shedeur.these are the names of the men who shall assist you. From Reuben, Elizur the son of Shedeur;
6Of Simeon; Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai.from Simeon, Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai;
7Of Judah; Nahshon the son of Amminadab.from Judah, Nahshon the son of Amminadab;
8Of Issachar; Nethanel the son of Zuar.from Issachar, Nethanel the son of Zuar;
9Of Zebulun; Eliab the son of Helon.from Zebulun, Eliab the son of Helon;
10Of the children of Joseph: of Ephraim; Elishama the son of Ammihud: of Manasseh; Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur.from the children of Joseph: from Ephraim, Elishama the son of Ammihud; from Manasseh, Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur;
11Of Benjamin; Abidan the son of Gideoni.from Benjamin, Abidan the son of Gideoni;
12Of Dan; Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai.from Dan, Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai;
13Of Asher; Pagiel the son of Ocran.from Asher, Pagiel the son of Ocran;
14Of Gad; Eliasaph the son of Deuel.from Gad, Eliasaph the son of Deuel;
15Of Naphtali; Ahira the son of Enan.from Naphtali, Ahira the son of Enan.
16These were the renowned of the congregation, princes of the tribes of their fathers, heads of thousands in Israel.These were the ones chosen from the congregation, the chiefs of their ancestral tribes, the heads of the clans of Israel.
17And Moses and Aaron took these men which are expressed by their names:Moses and Aaron took these men who had been named,
18And they assembled all the congregation together on the first day of the second month, and they declared their pedigrees after their families, by the house of their fathers, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, by their polls.on the first day of the second month they assembled the whole congregation, who registered themselves by clans, by ancestral houses, according to the number of names from twenty years old and upward, head by head.
19As the LORD commanded Moses, so he numbered them in the wilderness of Sinai.As the LORD commanded Moses, so he listed them in the wilderness of Sinai.
20And the children of Reuben, Israel's eldest son, by their generations, after their families, by the house of their fathers, according to the number of the names, by their polls, every male from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war;The people of Reuben, Israel's firstborn, their generations, by their clans, by their ancestral houses, according to the number of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
21Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Reuben, were forty and six thousand and five hundred.those listed of the tribe of Reuben were 46,500.
22Of the children of Simeon, by their generations, after their families, by the house of their fathers, those that were numbered of them, according to the number of the names, by their polls, every male from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war;The people of Simeon, their generations, by their clans, by their ancestral houses, those of them who were listed, according to the number of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
23Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Simeon, were fifty and nine thousand and three hundred.those listed of the tribe of Simeon were 59,300.
24Of the children of Gad, by their generations, after their families, by the house of their fathers, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war;The people of Gad, their generations, by their clans, by their ancestral houses, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
25Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Gad, were forty and five thousand six hundred and fifty.those listed of the tribe of Gad were 45,650.
26Of the children of Judah, by their generations, after their families, by the house of their fathers, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war;The people of Judah, their generations, by their clans, by their ancestral houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
27Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Judah, were threescore and fourteen thousand and six hundred.those listed of the tribe of Judah were 74,600.
28Of the children of Issachar, by their generations, after their families, by the house of their fathers, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war;The people of Issachar, their generations, by their clans, by their ancestral houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
29Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Issachar, were fifty and four thousand and four hundred.those listed of the tribe of Issachar were 54,400.
30Of the children of Zebulun, by their generations, after their families, by the house of their fathers, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war;The people of Zebulun, their generations, by their clans, by their ancestral houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
31Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Zebulun, were fifty and seven thousand and four hundred.those listed of the tribe of Zebulun were 57,400.
32Of the children of Joseph, namely, of the children of Ephraim, by their generations, after their families, by the house of their fathers, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war;The people of Joseph, namely the people of Ephraim, their generations, by their clans, by their ancestral houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
33Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Ephraim, were forty thousand and five hundred.those listed of the tribe of Ephraim were 40,500.
34Of the children of Manasseh, by their generations, after their families, by the house of their fathers, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war;The people of Manasseh, their generations, by their clans, by their ancestral houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
35Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Manasseh, were thirty and two thousand and two hundred.those listed of the tribe of Manasseh were 32,200.
36Of the children of Benjamin, by their generations, after their families, by the house of their fathers, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war;The people of Benjamin, their generations, by their clans, by their ancestral houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
37Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Benjamin, were thirty and five thousand and four hundred.those listed of the tribe of Benjamin were 35,400.
38Of the children of Dan, by their generations, after their families, by the house of their fathers, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war;The people of Dan, their generations, by their clans, by their ancestral houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
39Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Dan, were threescore and two thousand and seven hundred.those listed of the tribe of Dan were 62,700.
40Of the children of Asher, by their generations, after their families, by the house of their fathers, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war;The people of Asher, their generations, by their clans, by their ancestral houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
41Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Asher, were forty and one thousand and five hundred.those listed of the tribe of Asher were 41,500.
42Of the children of Naphtali, throughout their generations, after their families, by the house of their fathers, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war;The people of Naphtali, their generations, by their clans, by their ancestral houses, according to the number of names, from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
43Those that were numbered of them, even of the tribe of Naphtali, were fifty and three thousand and four hundred.those listed of the tribe of Naphtali were 53,400.
44These are those that were numbered, which Moses and Aaron numbered, and the princes of Israel, being twelve men: each one was for the house of his fathers.These are those who were listed, whom Moses and Aaron listed with the help of the chiefs of Israel, twelve men, each representing his ancestral house.
45So were all those that were numbered of the children of Israel, by the house of their fathers, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war in Israel;So all those listed of the children of Israel, by their ancestral houses, from twenty years old and upward, every man able to go to war in Israel —
46Even all they that were numbered were six hundred thousand and three thousand and five hundred and fifty.all those listed were 603,550.
47But the Levites after the tribe of their fathers were not numbered among them.But the Levites were not listed along with them by their ancestral tribe.
48For the LORD had spoken unto Moses, saying,For the LORD spoke to Moses, saying,
49Only thou shalt not number the tribe of Levi, neither take the sum of them among the children of Israel:'Only the tribe of Levi You shall not list, and You shall not take a census of them among the children of Israel.
50But thou shalt appoint the Levites over the tabernacle of testimony, and over all the vessels thereof, and over all things that belong to it: they shall bear the tabernacle, and all the vessels thereof; and they shall minister unto it, and shall encamp round about the tabernacle.But appoint the Levites over the tabernacle of the testimony, and over all its furnishings, and over all that belongs to it. They shall carry the tabernacle and all its furnishings. They shall take care of it and shall camp around the tabernacle.
51And when the tabernacle setteth forward, the Levites shall take it down: and when the tabernacle is to be pitched, the Levites shall set it up: and the stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death.When the tabernacle is to set out, the Levites shall take it down, and when the tabernacle is to be pitched, the Levites shall set it up. And if any outsider comes near, He shall be put to death.
52And the children of Israel shall pitch their tents, every man by his own camp, and every man by his own standard, throughout their hosts.The children of Israel shall pitch their tents by their companies, every man in his own camp and every man by his own standard.
53But the Levites shall pitch round about the tabernacle of testimony, that there be no wrath upon the congregation of the children of Israel: and the Levites shall keep the charge of the tabernacle of testimony.But the Levites shall camp around the tabernacle of the testimony, so that there may be no wrath on the congregation of the children of Israel. And the Levites shall keep guard over the tabernacle of the testimony.'
54And the children of Israel did according to all that the LORD commanded Moses, so did they.Thus the children of Israel did; according to all that the LORD commanded Moses, so they did.
