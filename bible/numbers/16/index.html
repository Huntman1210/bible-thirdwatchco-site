Numbers 16

Korah, Dathan, and Abiram lead a rebellion of 250 leaders against the authority of Moses and Aaron. The LORD causes the earth to open and swallow Korah's company alive, and fire from the LORD consumes the 250 men offering incense. The next day the congregation grumbles again, and a plague kills 14,700 before Aaron makes atonement.
