Amos 2

The LORD continues His judgments against Moab and then turns to Judah and Israel. Israel is condemned for selling the righteous for silver and the poor for a pair of shoes, for oppressing the afflicted, and for profaning the LORD's holy name. God reminds them He brought them out of Egypt and raised up prophets among them — yet they silenced the prophets and corrupted the Nazarites.
